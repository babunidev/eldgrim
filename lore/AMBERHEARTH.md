# Amberhearth

**Status:** Established foundation; customs and details continue to develop.

Amberhearth is a fellowship initiated by Eldgrim and conceived as a home for travelers, stories, and companionship. Its spirit is one of welcome and shared experience rather than pressure to perform or prove oneself.

Eldgrim stands outside the fellowship's conventional rank structure.

The fellowship was founded by Jasmine Amberbrew, who gathered signatures in Kharanos after hearing a voice at the campfires.

## Rank names
The current recorded rank names are:
1. Ember
2. Wayfarer
3. Kindler
4. Hearthkeeper
5. Hearthwarden

This document records the names only. It does not assign duties, privileges, requirements, or progression rules.

## Project boundary
This is a character-lore reference, not the guild's operational charter. Governance, recruitment, event planning, and member procedures belong in the Amberhearth/ROARguild project documentation.
