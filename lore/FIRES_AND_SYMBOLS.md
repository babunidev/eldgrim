# Fires and Symbols

**Status:** Developing.

Fire and the hearth are central motifs in the public identity of Amberhearth: warmth, gathering, welcome, shared stories, and the small moments that make a journey meaningful.

Eldgrim's association with these motifs is part of the character's public presentation. Specific metaphysical explanations, powers, rules, or hidden meanings must not be added without an explicit canon decision.

## Motif guidance
- A fire can be a meeting point rather than merely a spectacle.
- A hearth can suggest welcome and belonging without requiring uniformity.
- Stories and companionship matter as much as the location of a gathering.
- Keep symbolic interpretations open where the creative has not settled them.
