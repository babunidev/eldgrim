# The world Eldgrim knows

**Status:** Developing. Outdoor level bands were read from the [ZugZugDB zone index](https://zugzugdb.com/zones) on 15 November 2026. Dungeon doors were read from that database's own zone pages the same day, and only where the page names a door. The nine Forever dungeons use the level ranges and doors in [Blizzard's dungeon note](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301455/explore-the-new-dungeons-of-world-of-warcraft-forever). The land column is a short picture of the ground and the weather, added 18 November 2026. Classic lands follow their long-known character. Riverglades follows Blizzard's description of a rugged frontier of rivers and grasslands. The client build was not recorded on the zone read. None of this was walked in-game.

Azeroth here is the Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor. Camps, caves, seas, passes, and unused maps from the same index are left off. A place that is not written here is not a place to send someone. A dash is a fact the source did not give. Do not fill a dash in. The land column is a picture, not a permission. A row with no level is known, and it is not an errand. A raid and a battleground are not an errand.

Amberhearth walks the Alliance side. An errand for an Alliance character stays in an Alliance city, an Alliance land, a land with no side, or a dungeon this page does not mark Horde. An errand for a Horde character stays on the Horde side the same way.
