ELDGRIM - an addon for World of Warcraft Forever
================================================

Eldgrim's Hearth for the guild Amberhearth: Eldgrim's weekly Ember, quests
that members set for each other, and a notebook for the quest you are on.

This is an alpha. Expect rough edges, and tell us in Discord when something
looks wrong.


INSTALLING
----------

1. Close World of Warcraft.

2. Open your game folder and go to:

       World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\

   If there is no AddOns folder, create it.

3. Extract the zip there. It already holds a folder named Eldgrim, so you
   should end up with:

       ...\Interface\AddOns\Eldgrim\Eldgrim.toc

   If you see Eldgrim\Eldgrim\Eldgrim.toc instead, move the inner Eldgrim
   folder up one level.

4. Start the game. At the character screen, click AddOns (bottom left) and
   make sure Eldgrim is ticked.

Updating: close the game, delete the old Eldgrim folder in AddOns, and
extract the new zip the same way. Your quests and notes are kept, because
the game saves them elsewhere (in the WTF folder).

Download the latest version from https://www.babuni.dev/projects/eldgrim/


OPENING THE HEARTH
------------------

Open the guild window and click the flame in its top-left corner, where the
guild portrait usually is. Or type:

    /eldgrim log

Escape closes it.


ELDGRIM'S EMBER (left column)
-----------------------------

Each week Eldgrim sets one Ember. It changes on Sunday. The card shows its
story and who has completed it.

You do not complete the Ember in the game. Hover the question mark on the
card to see how: in short, post a screenshot in the Eldgrim channel on
Discord with @Eldgrim and the word "ember". When another Amberhearth member
Roars it, Eldgrim adds you to the ones who completed it within a day or two.


MEMBER QUESTS (middle and right columns)
----------------------------------------

The middle column lists open quests set by members. Click one to read it on
the right.

Setting a quest:
  - Click "Create quest". Write a title, a story and an objective.
  - Choose who can complete it:
      Everyone, once each - every member can do it once.
      One member          - the first to accept takes it off the board.
  - You can have up to 3 quests at a time. You cannot accept your own.
  - The quest stays until you delete it.

Doing a quest:
  - Click "Accept". You can be on one quest at a time.
  - When it is done, click "Mark complete". The quest's creator then
    confirms it, and it is completed.
  - Changed your mind? "Abandon" gives it up. A one-member quest goes back
    on the board for others.

There is no proof to attach in the game: you and the quest's creator settle
it between you. Member quests are for fun and roleplay; they give no points.


THE QUEST NOTEBOOK
------------------

While you are on a quest, the middle column becomes your notebook. Type a
note in the field at the bottom and press Enter. Click the green check to
remove a note when you are done with it.

Notes stay on your computer and are never sent to anyone. They are cleared
when you finish or abandon the quest, or when its creator deletes it.
"Board" shows the quest list again; "Notebook" brings you back.


INVITING SOMEONE
----------------

Target a player and type /eldgrim on its own. You whisper them a short
invitation to Amberhearth.


TROUBLE?
--------

- No flame on the guild window: check that Eldgrim is ticked under AddOns,
  then restart the game fully (a /reload is not enough after an update).
- The board is empty: quests and the Ember arrive from other members who
  have the addon. Give it a minute after logging in.

Ask in the Amberhearth Discord if you are stuck.
