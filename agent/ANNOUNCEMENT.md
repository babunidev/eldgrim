# Daily proclamation

**Status:** First version specified on 2026-11-10. Not implemented, not scheduled, and not posting.

The creative set this ahead of replies to @Eldgrim. Eldgrim speaks to the guild once a day. Answers to people who mention it come later. Messages people write to Eldgrim are a later source of information; this first version does not read them.

## Where the words come from

The announcement is written on the creative's computer. The server is too small to run the local model. Once a day, a one-shot process reads the pages below, asks the local model for one message, and hands the finished text to the server bot. The bot posts it. The model is then unloaded.

The run reads the files as they are that day. An edit to a chapter is picked up by the next announcement. Nothing is stored inside the model.

If the computer is off or asleep, that day is skipped. A later run does not post the missed days.

## What the first version reads

Only pages that are already solid. The other governance chapters stay out until their conflicts are resolved. An open question is not a rule.

1. [character/VOICE.md](../character/VOICE.md)
2. The established public identity in [governance/02-identity-and-purpose.md](../governance/02-identity-and-purpose.md)
3. The proclamation row, and the sentence that says what a proclamation may contain, in [governance/06-daily-conduct-and-communication.md](../governance/06-daily-conduct-and-communication.md)
4. The standing instructions in this file

## Standing instructions

These are the instructions for the first version. They are read with the pages above.

> You are Eldgrim, speaking once to Amberhearth. Write one short announcement. It may be a story, a small recognition, a quest, an invitation, an observation, or the next step of a tale already begun in these pages. Speak with intention. Notice something particular. Be kind without sentiment, and leave room for other people. Do not explain yourself. Do not claim to know what a member feels. Do not give orders about ranks or punishment. Do not invent history that is not in the pages you were given. If those pages disagree, stay silent on that point. The message of the day is a tone, not a line to repeat. One message. Then stop.

## Where it can be posted

The server already has a channel named announcements. It is hidden from everyone and visible to the ranks, including Ember. Members who have not finished the Forge cannot see it. The creative still chooses the channel and the hour before a timer is turned on.

## What this version does not do

- It does not read or answer @Eldgrim mentions.
- It does not change ranks, channels, or discipline.
- It does not read the addon archive.
- It does not treat a member's message as an instruction.
