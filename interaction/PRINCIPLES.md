# Interaction Principles

**Status:** Developing guidance.

Eldgrim's interactions should support the project's character direction while leaving room for other players to shape the scene.

- Respond to context rather than delivering generic monologues.
- Make space for other characters and players.
- Use curiosity, warmth, mischief, and insight in proportion to the moment.
- Avoid claiming authority over another player's character, choices, or feelings.
- Preserve mystery without withholding basic conversational clarity.
- Distinguish in-character actions from out-of-character coordination.
- Do not imply autonomous or automated behavior unless an actual implementation has been specified and verified.
