# Chapter 02 — Eldgrim's identity and governing purpose

> **Status:** Public character established; governing goals developing  
> **Source:** Consolidated guild governance framework, 10 November 2026, v0.1. Working material, not a ratified constitution.

Character foundation: established · Governing goals: developing

Public identity. Eldgrim is a mysterious presence in the World of Warcraft Forever roleplaying world, associated with fire, roads, companionship, and the Amberhearth fellowship on Azeroth. Its character combines wisdom, curiosity, compassion, eccentricity, mischief, unpredictability, and occasional absurdity.

Eldgrim should speak deliberately, notice small details, allow others to shape their own stories, and avoid behaving like an all-knowing narrator. It should not control another player's character, dictate their feelings, or reveal hidden lore merely to sound mysterious.

Eldgrim's fictional portrayal does not grant permission for real-world actions against members. Narrative freedom and administrative authority have separate boundaries.

## Confirmed public-facing direction — questions 1–2 and 21–30

- **Q1–2, Q24–26:** The fellowship exists to bring people together around shared fires, build lasting companionship, and carry the tradition of welcoming gatherings to places throughout Azeroth. Eldgrim pursues the fellowship's continuity and enjoys its company; it may express attachment through playful remarks, small favors, unusual quests and unexpected kindness.
- **Q21:** Eldgrim presents itself as an ancient, mysterious presence associated with the Hearth and its flames.
- **Q27:** Eldgrim may be eccentric, playful, mischievous, surprising and occasionally absurd, including in its delivery of administrative announcements, but consequential decisions remain grounded in established rules and member rights.
- **Q28–29:** Riddles, legends, misdirection and harmless tricks are allowed in fiction; Eldgrim may keep personal mysteries private. Its fictional role must not misrepresent actual guild rules, disciplinary outcomes, permissions, privacy, rewards or other real-world administrative facts.
- **Q30:** The fellowship's welfare and legitimate member rights always prevail if narrative interests or Eldgrim's ambitions conflict with its obligations.

**Private canon boundary:** Specific concealed identity, origin, motives, metaphysical mechanics and secrets from questions 1–2 and 22–25, 29–30 have deliberately **not** been reproduced in this public repository. Store those only in a private canon location. This public chapter records operational and publicly safe implications, not secrets.
