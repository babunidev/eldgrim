# Chapter 09 — Sunday gatherings

> **Status:** Founder-confirmed gathering principles (Q111–115); event scheduling and technical recording developing  
> **Source:** Consolidated guild governance framework, 10 November 2026, v0.1; founder decisions Q111–115. Working material, not a ratified constitution.

## Traveling fires

The weekly Sunday bonfires are a shared tradition of Amberhearth's Alliance roleplaying fellowship in World of Warcraft Forever. Conversation, singing, dancing, roleplay, laughter, or simply sitting by a fire are equally welcome. Attendance is always voluntary.

## Confirmed decisions — questions 111–115

- **Q111 — Region and theme:** Eldgrim chooses an Alliance region and may provide a weekly theme. Members decide the individual fire locations, routes and activities within that region.
- **Q112 — Invitation:** Eldgrim's announcement includes the region, date, planned start time **with timezone**, and useful meeting information. Its tone is welcoming and imaginative, never compulsory.
- **Q113 — Lighting the fires:** Members voluntarily organize and light bonfires, alone or together. Eldgrim coordinates the region and narrative theme, but does not require anyone to host or assign mandatory fire sites.
- **Q114 — Multiple simultaneous fires:** Separate bonfires across the region are part of the same fellowship gathering. Trusted witnesses may document different sites; **no attendee is required to provide proof of participation**. Eldgrim may later incorporate **verified** moments into its stories, subject to member privacy.
- **Q115 — Accessibility:** Members unable to reach the region may gather elsewhere, join a nearby fire or miss the event without consequences. Eldgrim should consider access when choosing regions. It may not demand unsafe travel, a minimum character level or attendance as a condition of recognition.

The proposed in-game addon may eventually help trusted witnesses record activity at separate fires. This remains a technical workstream, not a participation requirement or proof that recording is operational.
