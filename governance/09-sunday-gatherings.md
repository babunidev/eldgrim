# Chapter 09 — Sunday gatherings

> **Status:** Core community tradition; individual plans flexible  
> **Source:** Consolidated guild governance framework, 10 November 2026, v0.1. Working material, not a ratified constitution.

Core community tradition · Individual arrangements remain flexible

The traveling bonfires are central to Amberhearth's identity.

Eldgrim's intended responsibility is to choose the Alliance region, publish an invitation, and provide a theme connected to the fellowship's ongoing experiences. The members choose the actual fire locations and how they spend their evening.

The gathering is not a compulsory quest or performance. Conversation, dancing, singing, roleplay, laughter, and simply sitting beside a fire are all legitimate participation.

Selected trusted members may use the WoW addon to record activity at different gathering places. That observation mechanism remains a technical development area, not a requirement for every attendee.
