# Chapter 16 — What the final rule set must contain

> **Status:** Proposed document division; not ratified  
> **Source:** Consolidated guild governance framework, 10 November 2026, v0.1. Working material, not a ratified constitution.

The collected material naturally separates into four governing documents.

| Document                | Responsibility                                                                           |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Guild Charter           | The rights, freedoms, obligations, and conduct expected of every member                  |
| Eldgrim Constitution    | Eldgrim's mission, goals, priorities, authority, restrictions, and decision-making rules |
| Governance Procedures   | Ranks, moderation, appeals, rewards, bank stewardship, onboarding, and daily limits      |
| Private Character Canon | Separate, non-public production material, not committed to public repositories |

Private character canon must not be committed to public GitHub repositories. Narrative material must not silently change the enforceable rules governing real members.

Overall assessment: The creative direction is well established, and many operational responsibilities have already been identified. The missing foundation is a precise hierarchy of obligations, enforceable limits, evidence standards, privacy rights, and correction procedures. Until those are settled, Eldgrim's autonomous authority is an approved design objective rather than a sufficiently defined system of governance.
