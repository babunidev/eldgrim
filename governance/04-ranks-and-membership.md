# Chapter 04 — Guild ranks and membership

> **Status:** Rank names and Forge live; progression rules open  
> **Source:** Consolidated guild governance framework, 10 November 2026, v0.1. Working material, not a ratified constitution.

Rank names established · Progression rules open

| Rank | Progression |
| --- | --- |
| Ember | Entry rank through the Forge |
| Wayfarer | Undefined |
| Kindler | Undefined |
| Hearthkeeper | Undefined |
| Hearthwarden | Undefined |

Eldgrim is outside this hierarchy. Higher ranks do not inherently make one member the superior of another.

The Discord onboarding design uses a Forge process. Newcomers accept the hearth rules, register their characters, and receive Ember on completion. Existing members also pass through the Forge, except the Discord server owner. The software accepts up to 50 character entries as an input safeguard, not as a guild membership limit.

The Forge was deployed and tested by the creative on 10 November 2026. Public guild channels are now restricted to registered ranks. Every member except the Discord server owner restarted at Ember, and Eldgrim is intended to determine subsequent promotions.

Still undefined are promotion and demotion criteria, how a player's several characters share standing, what practical permissions accompany each rank.

## Uncertain character ownership — question 158

Character names and shared guild messages do not by themselves prove which player owns a character. Where a match is disputed or uncertain, do **not** merge player records, transfer standing/rank, attribute misconduct or grant sensitive permissions. Seek proportionate verification through Forge declarations, authorized witnesses or in-game checks, then correct any mistaken association. This extends Q85 and is **confirmed working governance**, not an implemented identity-linking feature.

## Delegated responsibilities do not follow social rank — question 169

Eldgrim may assign specific, revocable event coordination, moderation-assistance or guild-bank custody responsibilities with explicit permissions and accountability. **No rank alone confers administrative authority**, and no delegation grants direct access to the founder's PC-local narrative memory database (Q138). This is confirmed working policy, not a deployed delegation-management system.

## Confirmed rank and membership decisions — questions 43–52

- **Q43:** Ember — welcomed fellowship member; Wayfarer — established fellow traveler; Kindler — someone who brings people together; Hearthkeeper — trusted contributor who cares for the fellowship; Hearthwarden — exceptional trust, kindness and service. Ranks signify recognition, not command.
- **Q44–47:** Promotions recognize helpfulness, kindness, reliability, creativity, companionship and positive contributions, never attendance quotas, wealth, level or gameplay performance. Eldgrim may promote spontaneously. Members may request consideration without penalty; Eldgrim evaluates documented conduct and explains its decision without shaming comparisons. Repeated requests do not improve priority.
- **Q48:** Members may refuse a promotion or request a lower rank without penalty.
- **Q49:** Demotion is reserved for serious misconduct, repeated rule violations or abuse of delegated responsibilities; it is proportionate, documented, explained and appealable. Inactivity and refusal of optional activities do not justify demotion.
- **Q50:** Administrative duties are delegated separately and can be withdrawn separately from social rank.
- **Q51:** Standing and rank belong to the **player**. All registered characters share that person's rank, while their identities and narrative histories remain distinct.
- **Q52:** Eldgrim is intended to maintain the authoritative rank record in the guild database and automatically synchronize Discord roles. Authorized human members apply in-game rank changes via an approved addon or manual process and confirm the result. **Design decision, not an implemented integration.**


## Confirmed membership and Character Forge rules — questions 82–89

- **Q82:** Newcomers must affirm they are at least **18**, accept guild conduct rules and Discord requirements, and acknowledge disclosed data practices. Roleplay and activities remain voluntary.
- **Q83:** Forge requires a Discord account, rules acceptance, and at least one declared character with name, faction, race and class. No real-world identity, level, profession, biography or backstory is mandatory.
- **Q84:** No guild membership limit on characters. The current 50-entry software safeguard prevents mistaken submissions; a supported process may add more entries. This does **not** claim unlimited registration is implemented in the current interface.
- **Q85:** Character registration is a declaration. Where ownership is disputed or needed for permissions, confirm reasonably in-game with an authorized witness or addon-supported method. Never ask for game credentials.
- **Q86:** Members may add, correct, replace or remove registered characters through a supported Forge or administrator workflow. Shared rank and standing attach to the person, not a character.
- **Q87:** A retired/transferred character is marked inactive or unaffiliated without automatically ending the person's membership or altering other characters. Historical records follow retention rules.
- **Q88:** Preserve the confirmed initial Forge rollout: all members except the Discord server owner restarted at Ember. Eldgrim is intended to decide future promotions. Later character registration must **not** reset earned rank.
- **Q89:** Declining or abandoning Forge leaves the person outside registered-member channels, with no disciplinary or standing penalty. They may complete onboarding later.

**Implementation boundary:** The Forge and initial rank reset are deployed; character edits, ownership verification, archival/retention and later rank synchronization must be separately implemented and tested where absent.
