# Chapter 11 — Guild bank and practical stewardship

> **Status:** Delegated stewardship agreed; practical procedures open  
> **Source:** Consolidated guild governance framework, 10 November 2026, v0.1. Working material, not a ratified constitution.

Delegated model agreed · Procedures open

Eldgrim chooses allocations from available guild bank resources. A trusted committee of higher-ranking members performs the necessary in-game transfers.

The committee is intended to be a custodian rather than a competing decision-making authority.

A daily addon snapshot of the guild bank was part of the original design. A full deposit-and-withdrawal transaction ledger was not required.

The practical rules are still missing: who belongs to the committee, how transfers are confirmed, how unavailable items are handled, how errors are corrected, and whether some resources are reserved for specific purposes.

Guild bank participation must remain voluntary. The bank cannot become a reason to demand gold or materials from members.

## Refinement queue

- [ ] How are allocations confirmed, declined, or corrected?
