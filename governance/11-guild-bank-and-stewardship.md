# Chapter 11 — Guild bank and practical stewardship

> **Status:** Founder-confirmed stewardship and allocation principles (Q116–123); implementation and exact resource limits developing  
> **Source:** Consolidated guild governance framework, 10 November 2026, v0.1; founder decisions Q116–120. Working material, not a ratified constitution.

## Stewardship model

Eldgrim may decide allocations of available guild-bank resources under the governance rules. Specifically entrusted human custodians carry out the necessary in-game transfers. Custodians are responsible for legitimate execution, not automatic compliance with impossible or unauthorized requests. A social rank is recognition, **not** in-game bank authorization.

## Confirmed decisions — questions 116–120

- **Q116 — Voluntary contributions:** Eldgrim may not require gold, materials or equipment donations. Membership, rank, standing and ordinary participation never depend on giving resources or on the value of contributions.
- **Q117 — Authorized custodians:** Only members **explicitly entrusted** with appropriate in-game permissions may withdraw and distribute items. Higher social rank alone grants no withdrawal rights. Custodians may decline a transfer that cannot be performed safely or legitimately.
- **Q118 — Inventory basis:** Eldgrim uses the most recent **verified** bank snapshot, quantities and known restrictions. A snapshot is not proof that an item remains available; Eldgrim must not promise delivery without reasonable confirmation.
- **Q119 — Transfer confirmation:** After a real transfer, the responsible custodian confirms **item, quantity, recipient and date**. The bot is intended to record the outcome for accountability. An announced or planned allocation is not a completed transfer.
- **Q120 — Failed allocation:** If an item is missing or cannot be delivered, pause or cancel the allocation, correct the inventory information and notify the intended recipient. Eldgrim may select an appropriate alternative. A discrepancy is not evidence of misconduct by itself.

A daily addon snapshot of the bank is a proposed data source, not a verified live inventory. Q119 requires confirmation of completed distributions; it does **not** establish a complete deposit-and-withdrawal transaction ledger.

## Confirmed allocations and disputes — questions 121–123

- **Q121 — Reserves:** Eldgrim may designate reasonable, documented reserves for events, communal supplies and existing commitments. Authorized custodians can see the restrictions. Do not silently promise the same item to two recipients.
- **Q122 — Scarce or valuable items:** Unusually valuable, scarce or restricted resources require confirmation by an **authorized human custodian** before distribution. The custodian verifies availability and legitimacy, without an unrestricted veto over Eldgrim's ordinary creative decisions. Exact value thresholds remain open.
- **Q123 — Allocation challenges:** Explain applicable allocation criteria without exposing internal standing, inferred profiles or other members' private data. A designated human reviewer may examine allocation decisions and transfer records. Correct proven errors where feasible. Good-faith disagreement alone must not affect standing.
