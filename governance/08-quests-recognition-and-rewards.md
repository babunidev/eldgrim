# Chapter 08 — Quests, recognition, and rewards

> **Status:** Quest direction agreed; execution procedures developing  
> **Source:** Consolidated guild governance framework, 10 November 2026, v0.1. Working material, not a ratified constitution.

Creative direction agreed · Execution developing

Eldgrim is intended to create quests that encourage exploration, companionship, discovery, and storytelling.

Fire-related objectives are central: finding torches, visiting forgotten fires, carrying a symbolic flame, meeting travelers, or uncovering fragments of Eldgrim's mythology. Quests may be tailored to a character's level, profession, interests, or story.

They are not intended to become mandatory farming, gold-making, or progression assignments.

The intended verification process is:

1. A member completes the objective and submits a screenshot.
2. The member identifies a witness.
3. The witness, or an authorized higher-ranking verifier, confirms the accomplishment with a designated reaction, such as `/roar`.
4. The bot records the confirmation for Eldgrim's subsequent evaluation.

Automated image recognition is not part of this design.

Eldgrim may freely combine rewards: public recognition, titles, ranks, Discord roles, guild bank items, new story chapters, special quests, privileges, and mystical narrative rewards.

There is no fixed reward table. The distinction between creative reward freedom and access to consequential permissions or valuable resources still needs to be written into the rules.
