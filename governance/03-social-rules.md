# Chapter 03 — The guild's social rules

> **Status:** Social values established; formal conduct procedure open  
> **Source:** Consolidated guild governance framework, 10 November 2026, v0.1. Working material, not a ratified constitution.

Established values · Formal code of conduct not yet adopted

| Rule                           | Existing direction                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| G-01 — Welcome                 | Newcomers, veterans, casual players, and dedicated roleplayers are welcome.                                                  |
| G-02 — Voluntary participation | Membership does not require attending events, leveling, raiding, or contributing resources.                                  |
| G-03 — Respect                 | Racism, sexism, harassment, bullying, and discriminatory behavior are incompatible with the community.                       |
| G-04 — Character freedom       | No member should be shamed for their race, class, specialization, talents, equipment, or playstyle.                          |
| G-05 — Roleplay freedom        | Roleplay is encouraged but not compulsory at every moment. Other players retain control of their own characters and stories. |
| G-06 — Cooperation             | Members can organize adventures and gatherings without waiting for Eldgrim's permission.                                     |
| G-07 — Conflict                | Disagreements should be addressed constructively rather than turned into public spectacles.                                  |
| G-08 — Recognition             | Positive contributions and encouragement are preferred over punishment as the ordinary means of shaping the community.       |

These principles establish what the guild is trying to protect. They do not yet define a complete enforcement procedure.

## Confirmed scope and member voice — questions 163–166

**Q163 — Notice of rules:** Material guild-rule changes must be announced clearly with an effective date, affected membership and a link to the current rules. Obtain renewed acknowledgement where necessary for materially changed obligations or data processing. A hidden rule is not binding.

**Q164 — Non-retroactivity:** Do not discipline members for conduct that was permitted when it occurred. Current credible safety risks may warrant proportionate **protective** measures under already applicable rules, with explanation and appeal; this is not permission to punish retroactively.

**Q165 — Guild jurisdiction:** Guild governance concerns official guild spaces, guild activities and conduct directly affecting the fellowship. Eldgrim does not police unrelated private lives, other communities or personal accounts. Reliable evidence of external conduct matters only for a direct guild safety or membership concern, under existing fair process.

**Q166 — Member proposals:** All members may propose amendments, report contradictions and criticize policy without retaliation. Eldgrim may collect and summarize ideas; only the founder may approve documented rule changes.

**Status:** Confirmed working-governance decisions, not yet adopted as a ratified ROARguild charter or implemented moderation rules.

## Confirmed member protections — questions 13–20

- **Q13:** Roleplay is fully optional as a membership and participation requirement. Members choose their own level of engagement; consent and conduct boundaries still apply.
- **Q14–15:** Existing guild conduct rules remain authoritative. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, threats, hate speech, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption, scams, doxxing and repeated disregard for boundaries are prohibited. Eldgrim interprets existing definitions; it cannot invent new offenses.
- **Q16:** In-character rivalry and conflict are allowed with the consent of everyone involved. Roleplay does not excuse out-of-character harassment or violations of personal boundaries.
- **Q17:** Members may criticize Eldgrim, disagree with it, and refuse its requests without retaliation, subject to ordinary conduct rules.
- **Q18:** Declining or skipping quests, events or gatherings never reduces standing, rank, access or privileges.
- **Q19:** Members may organize their own activities without approval. Eldgrim's event-related responsibility concerns the Sunday evening gathering; member-led events remain independent.
- **Q20:** Suspension or removal is reserved for serious misconduct or repeated rule violations, with proportionate sanctions, documented reasons and an opportunity to appeal. Severe threats to safety may justify immediate removal.

Final investigation, review and appeal procedures still require decisions in Chapter 10.
